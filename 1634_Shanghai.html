📌 Travel Notes (HK → Mainland China)

  • 🪪 Permit, not passport: As a HK resident you need a valid Home Return Permit (回乡证) to enter mainland China — check it isn't expired. Visitors on other passports must confirm visa / visa-free eligibility before booking.
  • 🔋 Batteries & power: Power banks go in carry-on only (≤100Wh fine, no checked). Mainland sockets are Type A/I, 220V — same plugs work from HK, but bring an adapter for any UK-style 3-pin gear.
  • 🚄 Airport → city: From PVG (Pudong) take the Maglev (~8 min to Longyang Rd) + Metro Line 2, or a ~50‑min Didi to downtown. Landing near midnight on CX302? Pre-book a Didi. From SHA (Hongqiao) Metro Line 2/10 puts you downtown in ~30 min.
