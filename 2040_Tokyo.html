📋 Travel Notes
  • Visa-free entry + Visit Japan WebHolders of an HKSAR passport get 90 days visa-free in Japan — no paperwork, but you must have a return ticket. Register on Visit Japan Web before departure and screenshot the customs + immigration QR codes; it cuts the Haneda/Narita queue by 20–30 minutes on a Friday night.
  • LCC baggage trap — UO622 & GK027Both recommended legs are low-cost carriers: the headline fare covers 7 kg carry-on only. A 20 kg checked bag on HK Express costs roughly HKD 250–400 each way if bought online, more at the airport. If you are travelling with more than a backpack, run the numbers — Cathay CX509 with 2 × 23 kg included may close the gap.
  • Power banks & Japan socketsPower banks are carry-on only, never checked: max 100 Wh without approval, up to 160 Wh with airline consent (max 2 units). Japan runs Type A sockets at 100 V / 50–60 Hz — your Hong Kong Type G plug will not fit, so pack a universal adapter. Note 100 V charges some devices more slowly than home.
Airport → city: Haneda (UO622 arrival) — Keikyu Airport Line to Shinagawa ~13 min / ~¥500, or Tokyo Monorail to Hamamatsucho ~15 min. Both run until ~00:00, so a 22:45 landing is safe. Narita (GK027 departure) — Keisei Skyliner to Nippori ~41 min / ~¥2,600, or Narita Express to Tokyo Station ~60 min / ~¥3,100. Leave the city by 16:30 for a 19:30 flight.
