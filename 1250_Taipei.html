📝 Travel Notes

  • Entry (HK residents): HK permanent residents use the 台胞證 (Taiwan Compatriot Permit / 入台證) — arrange the online 網簽 before departure; it's quick but not visa-free, so don't leave it to the airport.
  • Batteries & power: Power banks/spare lithium batteries carry-on only, never checked. Taipei uses Type A/B sockets, 110V — HK's 220V chargers need a plug adapter (most phone/laptop bricks are dual-voltage, just check the label).
  • Airport → city & dumpling logistics: Taoyuan (TPE) → downtown via Airport MRT ~38 min to Taipei Main (NT$150). The classic xiaolongbao pilgrimage: Din Tai Fung 鼎泰豐 (Xinyi flagship) — go off-peak or expect a queue. Cathay economy HKG⇄TPE includes 30kg checked baggage, plenty for a weekend.
