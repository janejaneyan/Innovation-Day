🕌 Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque — Driving There & Access

📍 Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque Center, Sheikh Rashid Bin Saeed Street, Abu Dhabi, UAE · ~30 min / ~30 km from Zayed International Airport (AUH)
  • Driving from the airport / city: Head onto Sheikh Rashid Bin Saeed Street (E10) — the mosque sits between the airport and downtown, so it's an easy, well-signed drive. Google/Apple Maps and local nav (Careem/Yango) all route directly to the visitor entrance.
  • Where to park (important): Do NOT drive to the mosque plaza. Park at the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque Visitor Centre car park (free) located beneath / beside the Wahat Al Karama side. From there a short underground walkway / travelator leads you up into the mosque. Follow signs for "Visitor Entrance / Parking P1–P3".
  • Best arrival times by car: Open Sat–Thu 09:00–22:00, Fri 16:30–22:00 (last entry ~21:30). Arrive early morning (09:00–10:00) or after sunset to beat both crowds and the 40°C+ heat. Parking fills fast on weekend evenings.
  • Entry is free but requires registration: Book a free timed entry slot online in advance at the official visit portal (szgmc.gov.ae) — walk-ins may wait. Bring passport/Emirates ID.
  • Dress code (strictly enforced): Shoulders & knees covered; women need a headscarf. Men: long trousers, no shorts. Free abayas are available at the entrance if needed. No tight/sheer clothing.

📌 Travel Notes

  • ✈ Visa: HKSAR passport holders get visa-free / visa-on-arrival (30 days) for the UAE — carry a passport valid 6+ months and a return ticket.
  • 🔋 Batteries & baggage: Power banks in carry-on only (≤100Wh). Etihad Economy typically allows 2×23 kg checked + 1 cabin bag — confirm your fare's allowance.
  • 🔌 Sockets & driving: UAE uses Type G (UK 3-pin), 230V — bring a UK adapter. To drive, an International Driving Permit alongside your HK licence is accepted for rentals; roads are excellent and drive on the right.
