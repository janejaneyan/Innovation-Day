📋 Travel Notes

  • ✈ Visa: Croatia is in the Schengen Area (since 2023). HKSAR passport holders get visa-free stay up to 90 days — no visa needed for a 1-week trip.
  • 🔌 Power & battery: Croatia uses Type C / F sockets, 230V — bring an EU adapter. Power banks must go in carry-on only (≤100Wh, no checked); each transit hub (FRA/DXB/IST) enforces this.
  • 🚆 Airport → city: Zagreb Airport (ZAG) to downtown is ~17 km — the Croatia Airlines shuttle bus (~30 min, €8) or taxi (~€25, 20 min). Croatia is a compact base for day trips to Plitvice Lakes & the coast.
