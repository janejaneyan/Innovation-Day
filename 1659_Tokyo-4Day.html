📋 Practical notes

Getting around

  • Add Suica to Apple Wallet before you fly — no card machine, no queue, tap and go on every train, bus and konbini.
  • NRT → city: Skyliner 41 min / ¥2,580 (Nippori) or N'EX ~60 min / ¥3,070 (Tokyo Sta., direct).
  • Odaiba ↔ central Tokyo: Rinkai Line + Yurikamome. The Yurikamome loop is worth one ride in daylight for the Rainbow Bridge view.
  • Taxis are clean and safe but expensive — NRT to Odaiba is ¥20,000+. Don't.

Weather reality check

26.9°C on arrival dropping to ~21°C by Day 4 — pack a light jacket for the evenings. Rain probability is only 20–27% all four days, but late September is still typhoon season. Re-check 72h out; if a storm closes in, CX will usually allow a free date change on the same booking class.

Exhibition-specific

  • Pre-register for a visitor badge — on-site registration at Big Sight is a 30–45 min queue.
  • Business cards: bring 200+, and receive with both hands. Japan runs on them.
  • Power: Type A, 100V. Bring a Type G→A adapter; chargers themselves are 100–240V.
  • Pocket Wi-Fi or eSIM — Big Sight's public Wi-Fi is unreliable on packed show days.
