Travel Notes

  • 🛂 Visa: HKSAR passport holders are visa-free for short stays in Japan (up to 90 days). Bring a passport valid 6+ months.
  • 🔌 Power: Japan uses Type A sockets (two flat pins), 100V. Your HK Type G plug won't fit — pack a Type A adapter.
  • 🚆 Airport → city: From Narita (NRT), take the Narita Express (N'EX) or Airport Limousine Bus to central Tokyo (~60–90 min). N'EX is easiest with exhibition luggage.
