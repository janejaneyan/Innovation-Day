📝 Travel Notes — 2 Checked Bags & Family

  • 🧳 Two large checked bags: Cathay Economy "Light" fares include no checked baggage — book the Essential/Flex Economy fare (or higher) to get the standard 2 × 23 kg checked allowance per adult. Confirm the child has their own paid seat + baggage allowance; infant-on-lap tickets get much less. Overweight/oversize fees at KIX are steep, so weigh both cases before leaving home.
  • 🛂 Visa & power: HKSAR passport holders enter Japan visa-free for up to 90 days. Japan uses Type A/B sockets, 100 V — bring an adapter (HK plugs won't fit); most phone/laptop chargers are dual-voltage. Keep spare lithium batteries & power banks in carry-on only (never checked).
  • 🚆 KIX → city: Fastest to Namba is the Nankai "Rapi:t" (~38 min); to Osaka/Umeda take the JR Haruka. With two big suitcases + a child, consider a pre-booked airport limousine bus (luggage stowed underneath) or a taxi — far easier than dragging bags through transfers. An ICOCA IC card makes local trains stroller/kid-friendly.
