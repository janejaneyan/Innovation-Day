📋 Travel Notes

1 · Entry — visa-free + Visit Japan Web

Hong Kong SAR passport and BNO holders get visa-free short-stay entry to Japan (up to 90 days) for tourism/business. Passport must be valid for the whole stay. Fill in Visit Japan Web (customs + immigration QR) before departure — it cuts the arrival queue to a few minutes at both NRT and HND.

2 · Power banks — carry-on only

Power banks / spare lithium batteries must go in cabin baggage, never checked. Under 100Wh: no approval needed. 100–160Wh: airline approval, max 2 units. Terminals must be taped or capped. Charging via USB on CX 777 / A330 is available at most seats.

3 · Airport → city · and power sockets

  • Narita (NRT) → Tokyo Station: Skyliner 41 min (¥2,580) or N'EX ~60 min — this is your arrival airport on CX504.
  • Haneda (HND) → Shimbashi / city: Keikyu 25 min (¥500ish) — the fastest option if you take CX548.
  • Ariake / Tokyo Big Sight: Rinkai Line or Yurikamome from Shin-Kiba / Shimbashi.
  • Power: Japan is Type A, 100V, 50/60Hz. HK Type G plugs need an adapter; most phone/laptop chargers are 100–240V so they work fine.
